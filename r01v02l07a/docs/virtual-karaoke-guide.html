What You Need

Already enjoying KARAOKE NIGHT in person? Inviting singers who can't be in the room is easier than you might think. If you can make a video call and play a video, you already have everything you need.

📱Smartphone, tablet, or laptop
📶Wi-Fi — strongly recommended over cellular
🔊Something to play sound through — your TV speakers, a Bluetooth speaker, a soundbar, or the home stereo
🎵Your backing track ready to go in KARAOKE NIGHT
🎤Your voice — no experience required!
💡 Tip: Get on Wi-Fi before joining. Cellular can work, but it's far more likely to give you audio or video interruptions partway through a song.
Join the Session

Your host picks the meeting app and sends you a link before the event. Tap the link and you're in — the steps below work whichever app they chose.

📱 iPhone / iPad

  • Install Zoom from the App Store if you don't have it
  • Tap the link, then follow the prompts

🤖 Android

  • Install Zoom from Google Play if you don't have it
  • Tap the link, then follow the prompts
💡 Tip: No Zoom account needed to join — just tap the link and go. If Zoom is already installed, updating it first avoids surprises. Zoom is only an example — your host may well use something else.

📱 iPhone / iPad

  • Make sure WhatsApp is installed and updated
  • Tap the video call link, or just answer the group call

🤖 Android

  • Make sure WhatsApp is installed and updated
  • Tap the video call link, or just answer the group call
💡 Tip: WhatsApp works well on mobile. For larger groups, your host will want to use its Group Video Call feature.

📱 iPhone / iPad / Mac

  • Tap the link and join — nothing to install

🤖 Android / Windows

  • Tap the link — it opens in your browser
  • No app and no Apple account needed
💡 Tip: Only the host needs an Apple device — everyone else can join from a browser. The invite walks you through the rest.
1
Tap the link your host sends
It opens in the right app automatically — whatever the group is using. If the app isn't installed yet, the link will offer to install it.
2
Allow microphone and camera
Your device will ask permission the first time. Tap Allow — you need both for karaoke!
3
If it asks about audio, say yes
Some apps ask whether to join with audio, or show a speaker icon to tap. Choose the option that turns sound on — otherwise you won't hear anyone and they won't hear you.
4
Turn your volume up
Max device volume so everyone in your room can hear the backing track.
💡 Tip: Most apps don't need an account just to join — the link is usually enough. If the app is already installed, updating it first avoids surprises.
💡 You're the host? Pick an app everyone can join whatever device they're on, and send the link out ahead of time. That's really all there is to it.
Your Turn to Sing

When it's your room's turn, you control your own show. The host coordinates the order but you run your own playback.

1
Host gives you the heads up
Listen for your name or watch the group chat — your host will let you know you're up next.
2
Check you're unmuted
If you muted for any reason, tap the microphone icon to come back. Make sure your device mic can pick up your voice.
3
Start your backing track
Play it from KARAOKE NIGHT. Crank the volume so it fills your room and carries through your mic to everyone else.
4
Sing!
No judgement zone. Own it. 🎤
5
Stop your backing track when done
That's the important one — only the current singer's track should be playing. Stay unmuted and enjoy the show.
💡 Tip: Set your phone or tablet somewhere it can see the singer — remote friends are watching through it. Your room's sound comes along with it.
Audio Tips

One simple rule keeps it all working: only one backing track plays at a time — the current singer's. Everything else stays live, just like being in the room together.

🎤 When Singing

  • Check you're unmuted before you start
  • Point a device at the singer — that's the camera your remote friends are watching
  • Backing track volume up — let it fill the room
  • Stop your track when you're done

🎉 When Someone Else Is Singing

  • Stop your own backing track — theirs is the only one that should be playing
  • Cheer, clap, sing along — be the audience you'd be in the room
  • Reactions (👏 🎉) are great too, on top of the noise
  • Only mute for genuine racket — a barking dog, a TV in the next room
💡 Echo? Usually it means a backing track is still playing somewhere it shouldn't be — check yours is stopped. If it persists, turn your speaker down a little or use headphones; the mic is catching your speakers and sending it back out.
💡 Backing track sounding thin? Most meeting apps have a noise-suppression setting that treats music as background noise and filters it out. Look in the app's audio settings and turn it down or off. (In Zoom it's Settings → Audio → Noise Suppression → Low or Off.)
Playing Backing Tracks

Any current browser will play your backing track just fine — Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari. Open KARAOKE NIGHT, find your song, and tap to play.

💡 Our pick: If you'd like a recommendation, we find Chrome gives the smoothest playback — especially if you want to send the video to a TV. Not a requirement, just what's worked best for us.
💡 Want it on the TV? Use whatever method you normally use to show videos from your phone, tablet, or computer. KARAOKE NIGHT works with your existing setup — there's nothing extra to install or configure.
💡 Just singing along? If you're playing sound through your device or a speaker and not sending anything to a TV, none of this matters — any browser is fine.
❤️ Not using KARAOKE NIGHT? Sorry to hear that but you are always welcome at the party! Just use your preferred karaoke player and join in — we'd love to see you!
Troubleshooting

Something not working? Try these first.

ProblemFix
Can't hear anythingCheck device volume. Look for a speaker or audio option in your app. Make sure you joined with audio turned on.
Others can't hear meCheck you are not muted — tap the microphone icon. Check the app has microphone permission in your phone settings.
Echo or feedbackTurn your volume down a bit or use headphones. Someone in the call has their speaker too loud near their mic.
Video is frozenLeave the meeting and rejoin using the same link from your host.
Call cuts off after a whileSome apps have a time limit on free accounts (Zoom's is 40 minutes). Your host will start a new meeting — watch for a new link in the group chat.
Can't find the linkCheck your texts and email. Ask your host to resend it in the group chat.
Backing track sounds muffledTurn it up louder in your room. Then look in your meeting app's audio settings for noise suppression and turn it down or off — it can filter out music.
